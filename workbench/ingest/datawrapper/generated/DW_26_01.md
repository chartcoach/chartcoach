## Guidance

Maintain a consistent color-to-party mapping across all charts, maps, and articles within a single report, dashboard, or publication. Strive for consistency with broader media conventions where they exist.

## Why

Readers learn color associations quickly. When they see a color used for a party in one chart, they expect it to mean the same thing in the next. Inconsistency breaks this expectation, forcing them to stop, re-read the legend, and increase their cognitive effort. Over time, consistency builds a powerful visual shorthand that makes visualizations faster and easier to understand.

### Core Principle

Don't make your audience think more than they have to. A consistent visual language allows viewers to focus on understanding the data's message, not on decoding the visualization itself.

## When it applies

-   When creating a series of charts or a dashboard about an election or political topic.
-   When publishing multiple articles over time about the same political landscape.
-   In any situation where a reader will encounter multiple visualizations about the same set of categories.

## Exceptions

-   A convention may need to be updated if a new major party emerges whose color conflicts with an existing one, or if a previously used color is no longer appropriate. Such changes should be made deliberately and applied consistently going forward.

## Trade-offs

-   Sticking to a convention might mean using a color that isn't ideal from a purely aesthetic or perceptual standpoint. However, the cognitive benefits of consistency usually outweigh minor aesthetic drawbacks.

## Signs of Trouble

-   **Internal Inconsistency:** Your own publication uses different colors for the same party in different articles or even on the same page.
-   **Reader Feedback:** You receive comments from readers who are confused about which color represents which party.
-   **The Double-Take:** You find yourself doing a double-take when looking at your own charts because a color doesn't match your expectation for that party.

## How to Improve

-   **Quick Fix: Audit and Align.** Before publishing, check all charts in the series to ensure the color mapping is identical.
-   **Moderate Approach: Follow the Leader.** Research the color conventions used by major, reputable news organizations covering that political region and adopt their system.
-   **Comprehensive Approach: Create a Style Guide.** For your organization, create and maintain a simple style guide that specifies the official colors for political parties in the jurisdictions you cover. Make it an accessible internal resource for all content creators.
