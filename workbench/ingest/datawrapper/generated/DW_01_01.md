## Guidance

If your primary goal is to compare the trends of individual series over time and their cumulative total is not meaningful, use a line chart instead of a stacked area chart.

## Why

Stacked area charts are designed to show how a total is composed of different parts and how that composition changes over time. The "stacking" visually sums the component values, which draws the reader's attention to the total. This adds unnecessary cognitive load if the total is irrelevant to your message. A line chart focuses purely on the trend of each individual series, making it a cleaner and more direct way to show and compare trends.

## When it applies

-   You are visualizing multiple time-series data sets together.
-   The values of the different series are independent and should not be added together (e.g., stock prices, temperatures in different cities, unemployment rates for different countries).
-   Your primary task for the reader is to compare the trajectory of one series to another.

## Exceptions

-   If the values represent parts of a whole (e.g., population share by continent, market share by company) and both the individual shares and the total are important, a stacked area chart is appropriate.
-   If the series add up to 100%, a 100% stacked area chart is a very effective choice.

## Signs of Trouble

-   **Meaningless Total:** The total height of all your stacked areas represents a number that has no real-world meaning (e.g., "total temperature," "total price").
-   **Comparison is the Goal:** Your chart's title or key message is about comparing series (e.g., "X overtakes Y"), but you are using a stacked format that obscures this comparison.

## How to Improve

-   **Quick Fix:** In your charting tool, switch the chart type from "Stacked Area" to "Line." All the series will now share a common baseline, making them instantly comparable.
-   **Moderate Approach:** After converting to a line chart, review your color choices and line weights to ensure each series is distinct and the most important ones stand out.
