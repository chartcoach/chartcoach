## Guidance

When the key question is which combination of parts can achieve a target threshold, create a visualization that explicitly tests and displays these combinations.

## Why

In many systems, the performance of individual components is less important than how they combine to meet a goal. In parliamentary governments, the ultimate outcome depends on which parties can work together to form a majority. A standard bar chart of party support forces the reader to do mental math to figure this out. A dedicated coalition visualizer answers the question directly, providing immediate insight into potential outcomes.

## When it applies

- In election analysis for parliamentary systems to show viable governing coalitions.
- In project management to show which combination of tasks or resources can be completed within a budget or timeline.
- In any scenario where you need to show which combination of components meets a specific goal.

## Exceptions

- In two-party or "winner-take-all" systems where coalitions are not a primary factor.
- When the number of possible combinations is astronomically large and cannot be presented clearly.

## Trade-offs

- This is often a highly specialized chart type that may not be immediately understood without a clear title and explanation.
- It moves the focus away from individual performance and onto group composition.

## Signs of Trouble

- **Mental Math:** The audience is left to mentally add up bars on a bar chart to figure out which combinations are viable.
- **Complex Matrix:** The chart is a complex matrix or diagram that is difficult to decipher, obscuring the simple "yes/no" answer.

## How to Improve

- **Quick Fix:** Use a simple annotated list, clearly separating the viable combinations from the non-viable ones, as shown in the article's example. Use color and icons for quick comprehension.
- **Moderate Approach:** For each potential coalition, create a small stacked bar chart that shows the combined share of the parties against a reference line for the required threshold (e.g., 50%).
- **Comprehensive Redesign:** Build an interactive tool where users can select parties to add to a potential coalition and see their combined vote share calculated in real time. This allows them to explore possibilities themselves and understand the dynamics more deeply.
