## Guidance

When the primary task for the reader is to accurately compare or rank the sizes of different shares of a total, choose a bar chart or column chart instead of a pie chart.

## Why

Humans are notoriously bad at accurately judging and comparing angles and areas, which are the primary visual encodings in a pie chart. This perceptual limitation makes it difficult to tell which slice is larger when values are close. In contrast, bar and column charts encode values using length along a common baseline, a task humans perform with much higher accuracy.

### Core Principle

Use visual encodings that people perceive most accurately for the intended task. For quantitative comparison, position along a common scale (length) is superior to angle and area.

## When it applies

- The main goal is for users to compare the sizes of different categories (e.g., "Which region had the highest sales?").
- You need to show the ranking of different shares.
- The differences between share values are small and require precise judgment.
- You have more than a few categories to compare.

## Exceptions

- When showing a single share relative to the whole, and that share is a simple fraction (e.g., 25%, 50%). In this case, the task is less about comparison between shares and more about seeing one part of a whole.

## Trade-offs

- **Prioritizes:** Accuracy in comparison and ranking.
- **Sacrifices:** The immediate "part-of-a-whole" visual metaphor that a pie chart provides. While a stacked bar chart can also show parts of a whole, it's a less iconic representation.

## Signs of Trouble

- **Indecipherable Slices:** Your pie chart has multiple slices that are very close in size. Viewers cannot confidently tell which is larger without reading data labels. This forces them to read and compare numbers, defeating the purpose of the chart.

## How to Improve

- **Switch to a Bar Chart:** Convert the pie chart into a bar chart (or column chart). Place all bars on the same baseline. This allows for easy and accurate comparison of their lengths, making it immediately clear which categories are larger and by how much. Sorting the bars by value further aids comparison and ranking.
