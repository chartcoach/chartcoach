## Guidance

To emphasize when one category's value surpasses another's over time (a change in rank or an "overtaking" event), a line chart is a more effective choice than a stacked column or area chart.

## Why

Line charts represent each data series as a distinct, continuous path. When two paths cross, it creates a highly salient and unambiguous visual event that directly corresponds to one value overtaking another. In a stacked chart, all but the bottom series "float" on top of others. A change in rank is represented only by the changing thickness of the colored bands, an effect that is much less direct and harder to spot than an explicit crossover.

### Core Principle
Use visual encodings that make the key event as obvious as possible. The "intersection" of two lines is a stronger visual cue for a change in rank than a change in the relative thickness of two stacked areas.

## When it applies
- When analyzing time-series data for multiple categories.
- When a key part of the story is that one category grew faster than another and eventually became larger in value.

## Exceptions
- If showing the part-to-whole relationship and the total value is more important than highlighting the exact point of the crossover. You might then use a stacked chart and add an annotation to point out the rank change.

## Trade-offs
- Using a line chart means you lose the ability to see the total sum of the categories at each time point.
- The chart no longer inherently shows that the categories are parts of a single whole.

## Signs of Trouble
- **A Buried Story:** The main takeaway is a rank change, but you have to explain it in the caption because it's not obvious from the stacked chart itself.
- **Subtle Thickness Change:** The crossover is visible only as a subtle shift in the relative thickness of two adjacent color bands.
- **Ambiguous Ordering:** It is difficult for a viewer to quickly tell the rank order of the inner segments at any given point in time.

## How to Improve
- **Quick Fix: Annotate the Stacked Chart.** Add a text label and an arrow pointing to the spot on the stacked chart where the "overtaking" happens.
- **Moderate Redesign: Switch to a Line Chart.** Change the chart to a line chart to make the crossover event visually explicit.
- **Comprehensive Redesign: Animate the Transition.** In an interactive medium, you could start with the stacked chart to show composition, then animate a transition to a line chart to tell the story of the rank change.
