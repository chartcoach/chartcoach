## Guidance

Keep all text horizontal for maximum readability. If your category labels are too long to fit horizontally on an axis, choose a different chart type or shorten the labels.

## Why

Rotated text is difficult and uncomfortable to read. It forces the viewer to either physically tilt their head or mentally rotate the words, both of which significantly slow down reading speed and increase cognitive load. Horizontal text is the standard we read every day, and deviating from it introduces an unnecessary barrier to comprehension.

## When it applies

- This is most often a problem with long category labels on the x-axis of a vertical column chart.

## Exceptions

- There are no good exceptions for rotating text to be read. It is a signal that a different design choice is needed.

## Trade-offs

- Avoiding rotated text might require changing your chart type from a column chart to a bar chart, which could alter the layout of your page. However, the gain in readability is almost always worth this trade-off.

## Signs of Trouble

- **The Head Tilt:** You or your viewers have to physically tilt your head to read the labels on your chart.
- **Cluttered Axis:** Angled labels are crashing into each other on a column chart's x-axis.
- **The "Solution" is the Problem:** You have chosen to rotate labels because they don't fit. This itself is the sign of trouble.

## How to Improve

- **Quick Fix: Shorten the Labels.** Rephrase labels to be more concise. For example, change "United States of America" to "U.S." or "Average Annual Income" to "Avg. Income." Check if acronyms are appropriate for your audience.
- **Moderate Redesign: Switch to a Bar Chart.** If labels are still too long for a column chart, immediately switch to a bar chart. This provides ample horizontal space for long category labels to be read naturally and is the standard, correct solution to this problem.
- **Comprehensive Approach: Re-evaluate the Data.** If you have too many categories for even a bar chart to look clean, reconsider your data presentation. Can you group smaller categories into an "Other" bucket? Can you show only the "Top 10" and provide the rest in a table? Or is an interactive, searchable chart a better format?
