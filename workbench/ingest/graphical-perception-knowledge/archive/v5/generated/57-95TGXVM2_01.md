## Guidance

When using a difference overlay to visualize the change between two series, ensure the chart displays the original bars for *both* series, not just one.

## Why

Showing only one of a pair of series along with the difference forces the viewer to perform mental arithmetic (addition or subtraction) to reconstruct the value of the missing series. This increases cognitive load, slows down interpretation, and leads to significant errors. Users in the study described this design as confusing and difficult. Displaying both original series allows for direct lookup and eliminates this unnecessary mental work.

### Core Principle

Minimize the cognitive work required to decode your visualization. Every mental calculation you force upon the viewer is an opportunity for error and a barrier to understanding.

## When it applies

- Any time you use a difference encoding (like an overlay) to show the change between two values.
- When it is important for the viewer to be able to look up or compare the absolute values of *both* original series, not just the change.

## Exceptions

- If there is absolutely no need for the viewer to ever know the value of the second series, and the only goal is to show how the primary series has changed from its prior value. This is a rare scenario.

## Trade-offs

- Displaying both series (e.g., in a grouped bar chart) requires more horizontal space than displaying a single series. However, the resulting gains in accuracy and comprehension usually justify the space.

## Signs of Trouble

- **Mental Math:** Viewers are pausing to "do the math in their head" to figure out a value.
- **Incorrect Lookups:** When asked for a specific value from the non-displayed series, viewers give the wrong answer.
- **User Complaints:** Viewers say "it's confusing to have one year and a difference" or that it's "difficult to imagine the previous year's value."

## How to Improve

- **Moderate approach:** If you are using a single bar chart with a difference overlay, switch to a grouped bar chart format. Keep the difference overlay, but now it will connect the two bars within each group. This ensures all original values are directly represented.
