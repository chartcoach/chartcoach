## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Configure an initial default view that is narratively interesting and acts as a jumping-off point for exploration.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The paper identifies “stimulating default views” as an under-used interaction design tactic analogous to journalistic leads: a compelling starting configuration can motivate engagement and orient readers toward meaningful exploration paths.

- **The Principle:** An engaging first state primes attention and provides direction for exploration.
- **The Evidence:** [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Enter an interactive narrative and feel compelled to explore further.
- **Data Type:** Interactive visualizations that otherwise feel like undirected tools.
- **Audience:** General audiences.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** When neutrality demands no emphasized starting claim or highlight.
- **Reason:** A stimulating default may be interpreted as editorializing if not clearly justified [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Time to curate an initial state and narrative “hook.”
- **The Risk:** If the default view is too specific, it may narrow perceived scope of the data [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Defaulting to an empty, fully general view with no highlighted pattern.
- **Why it fails:** Users may not know what is interesting or what to do next [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** First-time users don’t interact beyond hover, or leave quickly.
- **The Test:** Ask users what seems interesting on first load; if they can’t answer, the default view is not stimulating [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Preselect a meaningful subset/time window and annotate why it matters.
- **Best Fix:** Design a lead-like first state that surfaces a key pattern, then provides prompts/controls to branch into related explorations [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].
