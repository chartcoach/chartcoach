## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Remove interesting but extraneous material (e.g., background music, irrelevant video inserts) from multimedia explanations.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Extraneous material triggers incidental processing that consumes limited capacity needed for essential processing (selecting, organizing, integrating the explanation), reducing meaningful learning.

- **The Principle:** Weeding / Coherence effect
- **The Evidence:** Learners show better transfer when extraneous material is excluded [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Learn the mechanism/process well enough to transfer to new problems.
- **Data Type:** Explanations where every second of attention matters (fast, dense sequences).
- **Audience:** Especially novices who have little spare capacity.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The “extra” material is actually essential to the instructional goal (not merely interesting).
- **Reason:** The paper’s coherence recommendation targets material that does not directly support the educational goal [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced entertainment/engagement “flair.”
- **The Risk:** Over-pruning can make the experience feel sterile, potentially affecting motivation.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Adding music or dramatic side clips to “make it engaging” without supporting the core explanation.
- **Why it fails:** It increases incidental processing and competes for limited resources [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users remember dramatic side facts or clips but can’t explain the core process.
- **The Test:** If removing the extra element does not change the causal explanation, it’s likely extraneous.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Mute/remove background audio and delete irrelevant inserts.
- **Best Fix:** Rewrite and re-edit the multimedia to be as concise and goal-focused as possible [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].
