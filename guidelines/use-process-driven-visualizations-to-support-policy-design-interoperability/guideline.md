## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

During policy design, visualize the correlations among topics and policy requirements within a process-driven workflow so requirements stay interoperable and consistent.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The paper’s simplified model states policy design “must visualize the correlating topics and policy requirements to ensure a new or revised policy’s functional interoperability” [@kohlhammerVisualizationPolicyModeling2012]. It also describes process-driven approaches as a way to create, monitor, and optimize policies through clearly defined sequences of steps.

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Draft or revise a policy while tracking dependencies and requirements consistency.
- **Data Type:** Policy requirements, related topics/issues, workflow steps and artifacts.
- **Audience:** Policy analysts and stakeholders collaborating in creation workshops and review cycles.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The “policy” is a simple, single-issue change with minimal dependencies.
- **Reason:** A full process-driven visualization may add unnecessary overhead.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** More screen space and design work for workflow/context views.
- **The Risk:** Users may feel constrained if the workflow is too rigid for real political processes.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Treating policy design as document-only authoring with no visual representation of requirement correlations.
- **Why it fails:** It neglects the interoperability requirement the paper explicitly ties to visualizing correlations [@kohlhammerVisualizationPolicyModeling2012].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Requirement conflicts are discovered late, during implementation or monitoring, rather than during design.
- **The Test:** Ask users to identify which requirements depend on a changed topic; if they must do it manually, the visualization is insufficient.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add a correlation/dependency view that updates as requirements are edited.
- **Best Fix:** Embed policy authoring inside a process-driven visualization that guides users through steps while continuously showing topic–requirement correlations [@kohlhammerVisualizationPolicyModeling2012].
