## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Write titles as if they will become the audience’s remembered “main message,” and verify the title’s stance matches your intent.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Viewers frequently echoed the title content as the visualization’s main message, and opposing titles on the same visualization led to opposing perceived messages.

- **The Principle:** Title-led message extraction
- **The Evidence:** 65% of recalled main messages reflected material covered in titles; title slant significantly affected perceived main message (χ²=27.06, p\<.001) [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Ensure the intended takeaway is what people retain and repeat
- **Data Type:** Visualizations shared standalone (e.g., social media) where the title may be the key interpretive cue
- **Audience:** Readers who skim and rely on top text for meaning [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018]

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Exploratory analysis tools where the chart is not meant to communicate a single takeaway
- **Reason:** Over-assertive titles can prematurely constrain exploration [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** You may need more iterations/reviews on title wording than on other annotations.
- **The Risk:** Over-optimizing the title can oversimplify nuanced evidence [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Assuming people will “get it from the chart” even if the title is slanted or incomplete.
- **Why it fails:** People may not remember the exact title text, yet still reproduce its message as their takeaway [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** The title makes a comparative claim that could be reworded to support the opposite stance using the same data.
- **The Test:** Ask a pilot reader to write the “main message” after viewing. If they restate your title, the title is driving recall—as expected [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Rewrite the title to match the takeaway you can defend as the visualization’s intended message.
- **Best Fix:** If multiple takeaways are valid, shift to a balanced/topic title and let the reader derive the conclusion, or present multiple framings explicitly [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].
