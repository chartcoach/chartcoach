## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Report uncertainty using a standard sequence: define key outcomes and measures, summarize variability, summarize internal validity threats, summarize external validity threats, summarize strength of basic science, then state an overall uncertainty interval.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

A standard format reduces the burden on scientists (by making explicit judgments they already consider) and reduces the burden on decision makers (by making uncertainty legible and comparable across topics). It also helps uncover uncertainties that otherwise go without saying [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Comparing and using scientific claims across studies or domains.
- **Data Type:** Any evidence used to support decisions (trials, models, observational studies).
- **Audience:** Mixed audiences who repeatedly consume scientific uncertainty summaries.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** A one-off exploratory conversation where the outcome and decision context are not yet defined.
- **Reason:** The protocol depends on identifying decision-relevant outcomes first; without that, the structure cannot be completed responsibly [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Upfront effort to adopt and teach the template across a community.
- **The Risk:** Rigid adherence can crowd out domain-specific uncertainties unless the template prompts their inclusion [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Standardizing only the final number (an interval) without documenting what it includes.
- **Why it fails:** Users cannot tell whether the interval accounts for validity and pedigree or just sampling variability [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Reports contain numbers but no structured account of what uncertainty sources were considered.
- **The Test:** Verify that each step (outcome, variability, internal validity, external validity, science strength, interval) can be located and read independently [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add headings for each step and fill missing steps with “unknown/not assessed” rather than omitting them.
- **Best Fix:** Implement the protocol as a required reporting artifact for decision-facing outputs, with a consistent layout across products [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].
