## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Present an overview organized by a single theme, and enable clicking/selection to drill into individual items for detailed narrative content.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The paper defines the drill-down story as reader-directed ordering: users decide which sub-stories to view and when, while authors pre-compose the candidate stories and their details. This format increases reader agency without requiring a linear path.

- **The Principle:** Reader-directed instance selection can organize narrative without linear sequencing.
- **The Evidence:** [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Explore multiple related cases (events, locations, periods) under one umbrella topic.
- **Data Type:** Collections of comparable instances (e.g., places on a map, events on a timeline).
- **Audience:** Readers who want to self-direct what they learn.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** You need to ensure every reader receives the same ordered argument or takeaway.
- **Reason:** Drill-down structures do not guarantee linear exposure to specific points [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** You must author and curate each instance’s backstory content.
- **The Risk:** Without summary/synthesis, users may consume fragments without understanding the overall conclusion [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Providing only an overview with drill-down but no thematic framing or takeaway.
- **Why it fails:** Users may not connect instances back to the theme [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users click around but can’t articulate what unifies the instances.
- **The Test:** Verify the overview communicates the theme and that each drill-down view clearly relates back to it (via messaging or consistent encodings) [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add a persistent theme statement and consistent visual encoding across overview and detail views.
- **Best Fix:** Add brief summaries/syntheses that reconnect explored instances to the overall narrative [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].
