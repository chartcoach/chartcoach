## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Do not separate corresponding verbal and visual information in a way that forces learners to hold one in working memory while waiting for the other.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Holding representations over time consumes limited working memory capacity, leaving less capacity for essential processing (selecting, organizing, integrating), increasing the risk of overload.

- **The Principle:** Reduce representational holding
- **The Evidence:** The paper describes overload when learners must hold verbal/visual representations across successive presentations, and shows benefits when this need is minimized [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Connect statements to depicted events without relying on memory.
- **Data Type:** Any explanation where cause-and-effect depends on linking two corresponding streams.
- **Audience:** Learners with limited ability to maintain mental representations during the lesson.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The instructional intent is to test memory/recall rather than teach understanding during the presentation.
- **Reason:** In that case, inducing holding demands may be part of the goal.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Less flexibility in sequencing; may require re-editing or redesign.
- **The Risk:** Over-constraining timing could complicate production.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Grouping all explanations together (a “big text/audio block”) and then showing visuals later.
- **Why it fails:** It forces representational holding and competes with essential processing [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users need to “remember what was said earlier” to understand what they see now.
- **The Test:** Identify every point where comprehension depends on recalling a prior segment; those are holding points.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Reorder segments so corresponding verbal and visual information are adjacent in time.
- **Best Fix:** Synchronize corresponding narration and visuals to eliminate holding demands wherever possible [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].
