## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Use color sparingly in tables to highlight must-not-miss rows/columns, distinguish categories, or mark highest/lowest values; use brighter colors for small marks like text and pastel backgrounds for large areas like cells.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Color is a strong attentional cue; using it deliberately helps readers find important information and navigate categories. Because perceived intensity depends on filled area, small colored elements need stronger colors than large filled backgrounds, as explained in [@muth_tables_2019].

- **The Principle:** Area-based salience control
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_tables_2019]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Identifying key rows/columns quickly; orienting within categorical groups; spotting extremes in a column
- **Data Type:** Categorical groupings (e.g., parties) and numeric columns with meaningful highs/lows
- **Audience:** General readers scanning rather than reading fully [@muth_tables_2019]

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The table is already easy to navigate (few columns/rows) and highlights would add clutter
- **Reason:** Unnecessary coloring can distract from the content instead of guiding attention [@muth_tables_2019]

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Visual simplicity and a neutral presentation
- **The Risk:** Overuse of color can make the table feel noisy and reduce the impact of truly important highlights [@muth_tables_2019]

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Using the same strong background colors for whole cells/rows as you would for colored text
- **Why it fails:** Large filled areas become overpowering; subtlety is needed for backgrounds [@muth_tables_2019]

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Colored rows/cells dominate the table more than the values do
- **The Test:** Step back or squint: if color is the first thing you notice rather than the highlighted information, reduce intensity/area [@muth_tables_2019]

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Convert strong background fills to lighter pastel shades; reserve bright colors for text-only highlights [@muth_tables_2019]
- **Best Fix:** Define a small set of intentional uses (highlight, category navigation, extremes) and remove all other decorative coloring [@muth_tables_2019]
