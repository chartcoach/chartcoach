## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

For decisions among fixed options, identify which uncertain quantities could change the chosen option, and communicate uncertainty only for those decision-sensitive quantities.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Sensitivity analysis answers whether any plausible value of an uncertain parameter would alter the preferred choice; if not, that uncertainty is immaterial for the decision and does not deserve attention in communication [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Choosing “which is best” among defined alternatives (treatments, portfolios, policy levers).
- **Data Type:** Predictions with uncertain parameters (means, risks, effect sizes).
- **Audience:** Decision makers who can apply their own values once informed.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The communication’s purpose is scientific completeness rather than decision support.
- **Reason:** Decision relevance is not the criterion for archival scientific reporting, even though it is for decision-facing summaries [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Requires understanding users’ decision context and options.
- **The Risk:** If you mis-specify options or users’ objectives, you may omit uncertainty they actually need [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Listing every caveat equally.
- **Why it fails:** It overwhelms audiences and hides which uncertainties could actually change the decision [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users cannot tell which unknowns matter and default to intuition or distrust.
- **The Test:** Ask “Would knowing this more precisely change the choice?” for each uncertainty you include; if the answer is “no,” remove or demote it [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Reorder uncertainties by their potential to flip the decision and label “decision-changing vs not.”
- **Best Fix:** Provide a sensitivity summary showing the ranges of key parameters that would change the preferred option [@fischhoffCommunicatingScientificUncertainty2014].
