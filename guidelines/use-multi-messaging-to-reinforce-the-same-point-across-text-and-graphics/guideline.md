## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Reinforce key observations with multi-messaging: coordinate text (left panel/captions/annotations) with corresponding highlights and transitions in the visualization.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The paper describes multi-messaging as providing related but different information across text and graphics, strengthening narrative comprehension (e.g., Budget Forecasts’ coordinated text panel and animated chart updates). It also observes that interactive graphs often under-use narrative messaging, making them feel more like tools than stories.

- **The Principle:** Redundant, complementary channels improve narrative comprehension and retention.
- **The Evidence:** [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand why a pattern matters, not just that it exists.
- **Data Type:** Dense charts where interpretation benefits from commentary.
- **Audience:** General audiences.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Expert analytic contexts where users prefer minimal annotation and maximal data density.
- **Reason:** Heavy messaging can be seen as noise or bias in reader-driven analysis [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Less room for data; more authoring effort.
- **The Risk:** Poorly coordinated messaging can feel repetitive without adding meaning [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Adding a block of explanatory text that doesn’t map to specific visual elements.
- **Why it fails:** Users can’t connect claims to evidence in the view [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Readers can quote the text but can’t point to where it is shown in the chart.
- **The Test:** For each key sentence, require a corresponding visible cue (highlight/annotation/transition) that evidences it [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add annotations tied directly to marks/regions discussed in the text.
- **Best Fix:** Synchronize narrative text changes with animated updates and highlighting that visually “proves” the statement [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].
