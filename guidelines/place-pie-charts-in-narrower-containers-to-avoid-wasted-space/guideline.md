## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Don’t give pie charts the full text width; place them in a margin column or sidebar to avoid unused white space. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Pie charts are not flexible in width like bar charts, so expanding the container often increases empty space around the chart rather than improving readability. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

- **The Principle:** Match chart layout to the chart’s aspect and scaling behavior to reduce wasted space.
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

This advice is designed for specific moments.

- **User Goal:** Read the chart without it visually dominating the page or creating awkward whitespace. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]
- **Data Type:** Any pie chart embedded in an article or report layout with variable column widths. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]
- **Audience:** Readers scanning pages where layout efficiency and hierarchy matter. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

List the specific scenarios where you should ignore this rule.

- **Scenario:** The pie chart needs to be a primary, full-width centerpiece graphic.\
  **Reason:** Layout hierarchy may outweigh space efficiency. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

Be honest about the downsides.

- **The Sacrifice:** A smaller container can limit label room if you haven’t simplified labeling. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]
- **The Risk:** If placed too small, slices and labels may become harder to read. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

Describe the common anti-patterns or "lazy fixes" people use that don't actually solve the problem.

- **The Wrong Fix:** Stretching a pie chart to full width to “use the space.”\
  **Why it fails:** The chart doesn’t benefit from extra width and ends up surrounded by unused white space. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** A wide chart area with a small pie in the middle and large empty margins. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]
- **The Test:** Compare the pie’s diameter to the container width; if much of the container is empty, move it to a narrower column. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Reduce the chart container width or move it into a sidebar/margin module. [@muth_pie_charts_2018]
- **Best Fix:** Redesign the page layout so pie charts live in narrow columns by default, reserving full-width space for charts that scale with width (e.g., bars). [@muth_pie_charts_2018]
