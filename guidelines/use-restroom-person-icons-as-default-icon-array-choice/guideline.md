## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

When you need one broadly effective icon for an icon array, use restroom-style person icons as the default.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

- **The Principle:** A single, familiar anthropomorphic symbol can improve both usability (preference) and retention (recall).
- **The Evidence:** Restroom icons had among the highest recall accuracy and were rated most preferred (highest mean preference score) compared with other tested icon types [@zikmund-fisherBlocksOvalsPeople2014].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Quickly understand and accept a risk visualization (clarity/helpfulness/liking) and remember the number.
- **Data Type:** Communicating a risk estimate as frequency out of 100 using colored “event” vs “non-event” icons.
- **Audience:** General adult audiences with heterogeneous numeracy and graph literacy.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Your product cannot display gendered or human-form symbols (policy/brand constraints).
- **Reason:** The paper’s strongest results are for restroom icons, but if such symbols are disallowed you may need another tested anthropomorphic option (e.g., head outlines) [@zikmund-fisherBlocksOvalsPeople2014].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced stylistic flexibility; a utilitarian look.
- **The Risk:** The icon may be interpreted as “people like me,” which may be unwanted in some contexts (the study also notes tailoring aspects) [@zikmund-fisherBlocksOvalsPeople2014].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Using novelty icons (e.g., smile/frown faces) to “make it friendlier.”
- **Why it fails:** Smile/frown icons were not among the top performers for recall or perceived-risk calibration patterns in this study [@zikmund-fisherBlocksOvalsPeople2014].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users comment the graphic is unhelpful/unappealing or fail to remember the percentage.
- **The Test:** Compare preference ratings and delayed recall between restroom icons and your current icon set; restroom icons should be competitive or better if the context matches this study [@zikmund-fisherBlocksOvalsPeople2014].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Swap current icons for restroom-style icons while keeping layout, denominator, and colors constant.
- **Best Fix:** Standardize on restroom icons in your icon-array component library and validate with a small A/B test measuring both preference and delayed recall [@zikmund-fisherBlocksOvalsPeople2014].
