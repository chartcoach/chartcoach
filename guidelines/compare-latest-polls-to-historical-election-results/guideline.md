## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Compare the latest polling numbers with results from previous elections in the same chart to show long-term trends and shifts in party support. [@muth_german_election_2021]

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Readers interpret “big” or “small” numbers relative to history. Showing past election results alongside current polls provides a baseline that reveals structural change (e.g., rising small parties, weakening large parties).

- **The Principle:** Context through historical baselines
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_german_election_2021]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand whether today’s standings are unusual or part of a long trend
- **Data Type:** Election results across multiple cycles + a current poll snapshot for the upcoming election
- **Audience:** General audience needing orientation beyond the current news cycle [@muth_german_election_2021]

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Your story is strictly about short-term campaign events within one year.\
  **Reason:** Long history can distract from the near-term narrative. [@muth_german_election_2021]
- **Scenario:** You cannot clearly distinguish “poll” from “result” in labeling.\
  **Reason:** Mixing them without clear labeling risks confusion about what is final. [@muth_german_election_2021]

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Space and complexity (more years/parties)
- **The Risk:** Viewers may treat the latest poll as equivalent to certified results unless clearly labeled as “latest poll.” [@muth_german_election_2021]

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Presenting only the latest poll without historical comparison.\
  **Why it fails:** It hides whether changes are meaningful relative to prior elections. [@muth_german_election_2021]
- **The Wrong Fix:** Adding many decades without a clear message.\
  **Why it fails:** The chart becomes a data dump rather than contextual evidence. [@muth_german_election_2021]

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** The viewer can’t tell what’s a poll vs. a past result, or what the long-term takeaway is.
- **The Test:** Remove the text around the chart; if the “trend story” (rise/decline over elections) isn’t visible, simplify years/parties or clarify labeling. [@muth_german_election_2021]

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Clearly label the current year as “latest poll” (not “result”) and keep a manageable set of past elections. [@muth_german_election_2021]
- **Best Fix:** Choose a historical window that matches your message (recent decades vs. back to the 1950s) and design the chart to make that message visually obvious. [@muth_german_election_2021]
