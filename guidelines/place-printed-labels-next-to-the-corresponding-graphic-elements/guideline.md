## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Put printed words/labels close to the part of the graphic (or animation region) they refer to; do not separate text and visuals across the screen.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Separating text and graphics forces visual searching and back-and-forth scanning, creating incidental processing that competes with essential processing needed for understanding.

- **The Principle:** Aligning / Spatial contiguity effect
- **The Evidence:** Integrated (nearby) text led to better transfer than separated text in the reported study [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Map descriptions to visual structures accurately while learning a process/system.
- **Data Type:** Diagrams/animations with multiple labeled parts and corresponding explanations.
- **Audience:** Learners who must coordinate text and picture to build understanding.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The graphic is so dense that placing text near elements would obscure critical visuals.
- **Reason:** Integration that hides the visual referent defeats the purpose of supporting selection of key image information.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Less visual cleanliness; may require more space or careful typography.
- **The Risk:** Overlapping labels can clutter the display.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Keeping a paragraph of explanations in a distant text panel while expecting users to “match it up” themselves.
- **Why it fails:** It increases scanning demands and incidental load [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users repeatedly look back and forth between a text block and the graphic to locate references.
- **The Test:** If you can cover the text and you can’t tell what part it refers to, alignment is likely poor.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Convert distant text into short labels placed near the relevant graphic parts.
- **Best Fix:** Redesign as an integrated presentation where explanatory text is embedded adjacent to the visual elements it describes [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].
