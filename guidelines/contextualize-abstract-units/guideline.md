## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->
When plotting data with large, abstract dimensions (e.g., millions of square kilometers), do not rely solely on numerical axis ticks. Add labels for known physical entities (e.g., "California," "Canada") directly on the axis at the corresponding values to provide scale.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->
Abstract numbers often lack meaning for the general public (e.g., "Nobody knows" how big 18 million sq km is). Physical comparisons anchor the data in reality.
*   **The Principle:** Relatable Dimensions
*   **The Evidence:** Footnote 3 in [@muth_readers_time_2017] highlights Derek Watkins' choice to compare ice area to the size of Canada or California directly on the y-axis, making the dimensions relatable.

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->
*   **User Goal:** Grasping the magnitude of large numbers.
*   **Data Type:** Large areas, distances, or volumes that exceed daily human experience.
*   **Audience:** General public lacking domain expertise in the specific metrics used.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->
*   **Scenario:** Scientific or technical reporting for experts.
*   **Reason:** Experts may find colloquial comparisons (like "size of Texas") imprecise or distracting.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->
*   **The Sacrifice:** Axis cleanliness.
*   **The Risk:** Choosing a comparison that is unfamiliar to a global audience (e.g., using "Size of Wales" for a US audience).

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->
*   **The Wrong Fix:** Listing the comparison in a separate caption text.
*   **Why it fails:** It requires the user to mentally map the text comparison back to the visual axis.

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->
*   **Visual Sign:** Does your y-axis only contain numbers (0, 5m, 10m)?
*   **The Test:** Ask a user, "Is this value a lot?" If they can't answer without calculating, they lack context.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->
*   **Quick Fix:** Add a text annotation pointing to a specific line: "This is roughly the size of [X]."
*   **Best Fix:** Integrate the comparison directly into the axis tick marks alongside the values.
