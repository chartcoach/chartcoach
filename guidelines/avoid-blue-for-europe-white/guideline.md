## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->
Avoid assigning the color blue (especially dark or royal blue) to Europe or White people.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->
Blue is culturally associated with professionalism, competence, royalty, and international bodies like the EU. Assigning this "positive/competent" color to the White category can subconsciously reinforce the idea of a "superior race." Conversely, other colors like red or black often carry associations of danger or negativity [@muth_race_ethnicity_colors_2024].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->
*   **User Goal:** Assigning colors to regions or racial groups.
*   **Audience:** Western audiences where blue has strong positive connotations.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->
*   **Scenario:** A map where water is not present and blue is the only high-contrast option available (highly unlikely).
*   **Reason:** Visibility overrides symbolism (but try to avoid this).

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->
*   **The Sacrifice:** You lose a common "flag-based" mental shortcut (EU flag = Blue).

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->
*   **The Wrong Fix:** Using a very dark navy blue.
*   **Why it fails:** This still carries connotations of authority and professionalism.

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->
*   **Visual Sign:** Is the "White/Europe" bar the most "trustworthy" looking color in the chart?
*   **The Test:** Check if you are using blue for Europe and warmer/agitated colors (red/orange) for other regions.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->
*   **Quick Fix:** Give blue to a different racial category (e.g., Black or Asian).
*   **Best Fix:** Remove strong blue from the palette entirely or shift the hue toward purple or turquoise.
