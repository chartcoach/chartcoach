## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Put annotations, callouts, and explanatory text immediately adjacent to the visual elements they reference.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

- **The Principle:** Reducing split attention lowers working-memory burden and speeds integration.
- **The Evidence:** The paper emphasizes that glances back and forth are effortful and that verbal guidance helps most when located near the relevant visual information [@zacksDesigningGraphsDecisionMakers2020].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Connecting a written claim (e.g., “unemployment exceeds target”) to the supporting pattern in the chart.
- **Data Type:** Any chart that requires explanation or emphasizes a takeaway.
- **Audience:** Non-experts who need help knowing what to look for.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The chart is extremely dense and nearby text would occlude critical marks.
- **Reason:** Occlusion can prevent verification by inspection, undermining comprehension [@zacksDesigningGraphsDecisionMakers2020].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Space and visual cleanliness.
- **The Risk:** Too many annotations can overwhelm viewers.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Putting a block of bullet conclusions far from the chart and expecting viewers to match them up.
- **Why it fails:** It requires slow, effortful integration and repeated scanning [@zacksDesigningGraphsDecisionMakers2020].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Viewers must repeatedly look from text to plot to figure out what the text refers to.
- **The Test:** Cover the text and ask what the chart shows; then show the text and ask which marks it refers to. If viewers struggle, co-location is missing.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add leader lines/callouts that point to the exact marks or regions discussed.
- **Best Fix:** Recompose the layout so each claim sits within the plot area near its evidence [@zacksDesigningGraphsDecisionMakers2020].
