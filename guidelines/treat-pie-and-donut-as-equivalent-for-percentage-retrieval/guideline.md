## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

If you must use a pie-style chart for reading a percentage, feel free to use a donut chart instead of a pie chart (or vice versa); prioritize other design concerns because their accuracy is comparable.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Removing the center (donut hole) does not materially reduce accuracy for percentage retrieval in the tested conditions.

- **The Principle:** The central meeting point (salient angle apex) is not required for accurate percentage reading in these charts.
- **The Evidence:** In retrieve-value accuracy results, baseline donut ranked essentially the same as baseline pie (very close performance; both were in the top of the ranking and not separated by significant-difference pairs), and donut was not worse than pie in the reported ordering [@skauArcsAnglesAreas2016]. This equivalence is captured in the collation for recommendation use [@zengReviewCollationGraphical2023].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Read a single segment’s percent-of-whole.
- **Data Type:** Quantitative proportion shown as one highlighted segment (two-segment stimulus in the study).
- **Audience:** General audiences where either form may be chosen for styling/label placement.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** You are not using a standard donut/pie wedge (e.g., you switch to an angle-only depiction).
- **Reason:** The “donut≈pie” guidance only applies to baseline donut vs baseline pie; angle-only variants performed worse [@skauArcsAnglesAreas2016; @zengReviewCollationGraphical2023].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** This rule doesn’t help you decide whether you should avoid pie/donut entirely; it only says donut and pie are similar to each other for this task.
- **The Risk:** Over-interpreting the equivalence beyond the tested retrieve-value scenario.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Assuming the donut hole necessarily harms accuracy and therefore forbidding donut charts categorically.
- **Why it fails:** The measured performance for baseline donut was comparable to baseline pie for retrieve-value accuracy in the study’s reported outcomes [@skauArcsAnglesAreas2016; @zengReviewCollationGraphical2023].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** No noticeable change in user errors when switching between baseline donut and baseline pie.
- **The Test:** Swap a baseline pie for a baseline donut in the same layout and test percentage reading; if error rates remain similar, you are within the evidence-backed behavior [@skauArcsAnglesAreas2016].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** If you used a pie but need space for a center label, convert to a donut without changing the segment angles.
- **Best Fix:** Choose between baseline pie and baseline donut based on layout needs (labeling/annotation), not presumed accuracy differences, for retrieve-value tasks [@skauArcsAnglesAreas2016; @zengReviewCollationGraphical2023].
