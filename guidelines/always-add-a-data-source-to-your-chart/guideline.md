## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Add a clear data source line to the chart.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

A source provides transparency and functions as proof, enabling readers to verify where the data comes from and even recreate or improve the work, as insisted in [@muth_better_charts_2017].

- **The Principle:** Transparency as credibility infrastructure
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_better_charts_2017]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Trust the chart, understand provenance, and potentially access the same data.
- **Data Type:** Any chart using external, official, or curated datasets (including company reports).
- **Audience:** Readers who may question accuracy, methodology, or timeliness.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The data source cannot be disclosed (e.g., confidential/internal constraints).
- **Reason:** You may need to provide a constrained attribution (e.g., “internal analysis”)—but Muth’s default expectation is to include a source for transparency [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** A small amount of space and the effort to track provenance carefully.
- **The Risk:** If your sourcing is sloppy or incomplete, it can undermine trust rather than build it [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Omitting the source because the dataset feels “obvious,” or burying sourcing only in surrounding article text.
- **Why it fails:** Readers may see the chart out of context (shared, screenshot, embedded) and lose the ability to verify it [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** There is no “Source:” line on the chart.
- **The Test:** Imagine the chart is reposted alone: can a viewer tell where the data came from? If not, add a source [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add a source name (organization/report) directly under the chart.
- **Best Fix:** Provide a precise source reference (publisher + document/report context) that enables a motivated reader to find the exact data used, consistent with the transparency goal in [@muth_better_charts_2017].
