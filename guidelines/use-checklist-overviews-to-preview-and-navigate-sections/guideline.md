## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Use a checklist-style overview (e.g., labeled thumbnails of sections) to preview what will be covered and provide navigation.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The paper describes checklist structures as establishing shots that set expectations, remind users what each section contains, and enable jumping to specific segments while preserving overall structure.

- **The Principle:** Previewing structure supports orientation and reduces memory burden across sections.
- **The Evidence:** [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand coverage at a glance; jump to a topic of interest.
- **Data Type:** Multi-section stories (multiple topics or “chapters”).
- **Audience:** General audiences, especially skimmers.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** A tightly authored, linear story where you do not want random access.
- **Reason:** Checklist navigation encourages skipping, which may undermine intended sequencing [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Additional design and screen space for the overview.
- **The Risk:** Users may treat the story as a menu and miss narrative connections between parts [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Listing sections without showing what they contain.
- **Why it fails:** Titles alone may not communicate content well enough to orient or motivate navigation [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users don’t know what’s coming or can’t find a previously seen section.
- **The Test:** Ask a viewer to name the available sections after finishing; poor recall suggests missing preview/reminder structure [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add a labeled section index.
- **Best Fix:** Use thumbnail previews + labels and sync them with a progress indicator during playback [@segelNarrativeVisualizationTelling2010].
