## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

When communicating treatment risk reduction across two groups with different sample sizes, add icon arrays (one per group) in addition to the numeric counts.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

People often overweight numerators (e.g., “50 deaths”) and underweight denominators (e.g., “out of 500”), producing systematic over/underestimation when group sizes differ; icon arrays make the part-to-whole relationship visually explicit and draw attention to denominators.

- **The Principle:** Denominator neglect (numerator salience over denominator integration)
- **The Evidence:** In two experiments, accuracy dropped when denominators differed under numbers-only, and accuracy increased when icon arrays were added, eliminating the denominator-neglect pattern [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Judge whether a treatment reduces risk and by how much (risk reduction understanding).
- **Data Type:** Two-group risks or frequencies with unequal denominators (e.g., treated n=100 vs untreated n=500).
- **Audience:** General audiences, including older adults.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The two groups share the same denominator (equal group sizes) and space is extremely constrained.
- **Reason:** The paper shows the biggest benefit when denominators differ; when denominators are equal, numbers-only performance was already relatively high [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Uses substantial display space (arrays of 100 or 500 icons).
- **The Risk:** If squeezed too small, icons may become unreadable and lose the part-to-whole benefit.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Showing only the dead-counts (numerators) as the primary visual emphasis.
- **Why it fails:** This reinforces numerator salience and preserves denominator neglect when group sizes differ [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** The display makes “how many died” obvious but “out of how many” hard to see.
- **The Test:** Ask a reviewer to state each group’s total (denominator) after a 3–5 second glance—if they can’t, the design likely still invites denominator neglect.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add an icon array for each group matching its denominator (e.g., 100 icons for n=100; 500 icons for n=500).
- **Best Fix:** Pair the numbers with icon arrays that visually encode the full population at risk and the affected subset, so denominators are unmissable at a glance [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].
