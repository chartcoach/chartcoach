## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Color only the most important data to match your point, and set secondary/comparison data in grey so it stays readable but visually quiet.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Color acts like a spotlight that leads the reader’s eye to the critical elements; grey provides structure and context without competing for attention, especially when multiple comparison series are present, as emphasized in [@muth_better_charts_2017].

- **The Principle:** Attention steering via visual salience (spotlight + backgrounding)
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_better_charts_2017]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Quickly identify the “star of the show” while still being able to compare it to other series.
- **Data Type:** Multi-series charts (e.g., several product lines over time) where one series carries the message.
- **Audience:** Readers scanning quickly who need an obvious entry point.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** All series are equally important (no single focal series), or the task is to compare all series equally.
- **Reason:** Highlighting one series would bias interpretation away from the intended neutral comparison [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Nuance—de-emphasized series may feel “less important” even if they still matter.
- **The Risk:** If the highlighted series is not actually the message, the color cue misleads readers [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Assigning distinct bright colors to every series by default.
- **Why it fails:** Everything competes for attention, and the chart loses a clear focal point; Muth recommends grey precisely to keep multi-series charts clean [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Your eye doesn’t immediately land on the series that supports the headline claim, or the chart looks “rainbow busy.”
- **The Test:** Glance at the chart for 2 seconds: can you tell which series matters most? If not, your color hierarchy is missing [@muth_better_charts_2017].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Convert non-primary series to grey and keep one accent color for the focal series.
- **Best Fix:** Reconfirm the headline point, then redesign the color palette so salience aligns with that point (accent for evidence, grey for context) [@muth_better_charts_2017].
