## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Add icon arrays (pictographs) in addition to numeric risk information when explaining treatment risk reduction.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Icon arrays make both the numerator (events) and denominator (total cases) perceptually explicit, which reduces denominator neglect—people’s tendency to focus on counts of events and ignore the total they came from. In probabilistic national samples, adding icon arrays eliminated denominator-size effects on perceived risk reduction and reduced inaccurate estimates, particularly among low-numeracy participants [@garcia-retameroCommunicatingTreatmentRisk2009].

- **The Principle:** Denominator neglect reduction via transparent part-to-whole representation
- **The Evidence:** [@garcia-retameroCommunicatingTreatmentRisk2009]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Judge how effective a treatment is (risk reduction) accurately
- **Data Type:** Probabilities/risks expressed as “X out of Y” for treated vs untreated groups
- **Audience:** General public, especially people with low numeracy skills

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** You cannot show the denominator visually (e.g., no room or format constraints prevent showing the whole).
- **Reason:** If the denominator isn’t shown, the icon array may fail to convey the superordinate class that mitigates denominator neglect [@garcia-retameroCommunicatingTreatmentRisk2009].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** More screen/print space and potentially more visual complexity
- **The Risk:** If poorly integrated, viewers may attend to the graphic but miss the intended comparison between treated and untreated groups

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Showing only the number of events (deaths) as icons without showing the full population.
- **Why it fails:** It preserves the numerator focus and does not counter denominator neglect [@garcia-retameroCommunicatingTreatmentRisk2009].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** People react mainly to “more black dots” without referencing how many total dots there are.
- **The Test:** Ask users to estimate risk reduction; if estimates systematically change when denominators change (despite equal true relative risk reduction), denominator neglect is still present [@garcia-retameroCommunicatingTreatmentRisk2009].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add an icon array that includes the full denominator and visually marks the events.
- **Best Fix:** Provide two separate icon arrays—one for treatment, one for no treatment—each showing the correct total population size and the event count clearly [@garcia-retameroCommunicatingTreatmentRisk2009].
