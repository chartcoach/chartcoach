## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

When your audience includes older adults, include icon arrays in addition to numeric risk information rather than simplifying to numbers-only.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Older adults were less accurate than younger adults with numbers-only presentations, but icon arrays improved accuracy without an age-dependent penalty; the benefit of icon arrays did not depend on age in either study [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].

- **The Principle:** Representation can reduce apparent cognitive limitations
- **The Evidence:** Icon arrays increased correct estimates for older and younger groups in both a within-subject (lab) and between-subject (web) design [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand how much a treatment reduces risk.
- **Data Type:** Event frequencies and derived risk reduction.
- **Audience:** Mixed-age populations, especially communications targeting older adults.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The medium cannot support legible icon arrays (e.g., extremely small print area).
- **Reason:** The mechanism depends on viewers being able to perceive the full denominator and affected subset; illegible arrays can’t deliver that benefit [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** More space and potentially longer viewing time than numbers-only.
- **The Risk:** Poorly rendered or too-small arrays may frustrate users.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Removing visuals for older audiences under the assumption that “simpler text” is more accessible.
- **Why it fails:** In the experiments, older adults showed more difficulty with numbers-only, and icon arrays improved their accuracy rather than harming it [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** A numbers-only display is used despite unequal denominators and an older target audience.
- **The Test:** Compare comprehension in a quick pilot: older users should improve with icon arrays; if not, the arrays may be illegible or incorrectly mapped.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add treated/untreated icon arrays that encode each denominator and the affected subset.
- **Best Fix:** Use the combined numeric + icon-array presentation shown to improve accuracy across age groups in both studies [@garcia-retameroIconArraysHelp2010].
