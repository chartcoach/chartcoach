## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Use color deliberately to (1) set the mood and (2) guide the viewer’s eye with clear highlights; keep most elements neutral so the highlights stand out.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Color influences how a viewer feels about a graphic and where they look first; selective, high-contrast accent colors create a visual hierarchy, while neutrals reduce competition for attention. This is presented as the core “why color” motivation in the guide [@muth_colorguide_2018].

- **The Principle:** Visual hierarchy through selective emphasis
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_colorguide_2018]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Notice the key message quickly; understand what is important vs. background.
- **Data Type:** Any (charts, maps, annotated graphics) where emphasis and storytelling matter.
- **Audience:** Broad audiences, especially when scanning/reading quickly.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The purpose is to show many categories equally (no single focal element).
- **Reason:** Strong highlighting implies priority; if everything is equally important, heavy accents can mislead [@muth_colorguide_2018].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced color variety; some elements may feel less “exciting.”
- **The Risk:** Over-highlighting can make the chart feel loud or biased if too many items compete for attention [@muth_colorguide_2018].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Making everything colorful to “make it pop.”
- **Why it fails:** With no neutral baseline, nothing stands out; the viewer’s attention is not guided [@muth_colorguide_2018].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Your eye doesn’t land on a clear focal point; all elements compete equally.
- **The Test:** Squint at the chart—only the intended highlights should remain prominent [@muth_colorguide_2018].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Convert most series/regions to greys and keep one accent color for the key point.
- **Best Fix:** Redesign the palette around a neutral base + a small set of intentional highlight colors aligned to the story [@muth_colorguide_2018].
