## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Pick one (or a few) categories to highlight in color. Render all other categories in subdued styling so they recede.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

If the goal is to communicate specific insights, not every category is equally important. Using color selectively creates a clear visual hierarchy so readers see the key evidence first, rather than being overwhelmed by many equally loud colors ([@muth_fewer_colors_2022]).

- **The Principle:** Visual hierarchy through selective emphasis.
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_fewer_colors_2022]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understanding the main takeaway quickly (what to look at first).
- **Data Type:** Many categories/series where only a subset supports the intended statement.
- **Audience:** Readers of articles, reports, or presentations where a narrative point matters.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The task requires equal attention to all categories (no “main” category).
- **Reason:** Selective emphasis would bias reading toward a subset and undermine the intended neutral comparison ([@muth_fewer_colors_2022]).

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** De-emphasized categories become harder to inspect.
- **The Risk:** Readers may assume the muted categories are unimportant even when they still matter for context ([@muth_fewer_colors_2022]).

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Highlighting too many categories.
- **Why it fails:** The hierarchy collapses and you’re back to a confetti-like result ([@muth_fewer_colors_2022]).

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Everything looks equally prominent, or the highlight doesn’t stand out.
- **The Test:** Ask: “What do I want readers to see first?” If you can’t answer in one category (or a tiny set), the emphasis strategy isn’t being used correctly ([@muth_fewer_colors_2022]).

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Choose a single highlight category and set all others to a neutral, less prominent style.
- **Best Fix:** Write down the statement you want to make, identify the key evidence categories, then highlight only those categories ([@muth_fewer_colors_2022]).
