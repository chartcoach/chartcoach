## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Before presenting a full causal system explanation, pretrain learners on the names and behaviors of the system’s components.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Pretraining reduces essential processing during the main lesson by letting learners focus on building the causal model rather than simultaneously figuring out what each component is and does.

- **The Principle:** Pretraining effect
- **The Evidence:** Learners show better transfer when they know component names/behaviors before the multimedia explanation [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand how a multi-part mechanism/process works.
- **Data Type:** Component-based systems (parts + interactions).
- **Audience:** Learners with low prior knowledge of the system components.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The audience already knows the components and their basic behaviors.
- **Reason:** The primary benefit is reducing essential load for novices; if prior knowledge is high, the added step may be unnecessary [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Adds upfront time and content before the “main” explanation.
- **The Risk:** Over-pretraining can delay engagement with the causal story.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Introducing component names for the first time only while they are actively changing in a fast animation.
- **Why it fails:** It forces simultaneous component-model building and causal-model building, increasing overload [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users can’t keep track of what parts are called or what they do while also following interactions.
- **The Test:** Ask users to identify components and their basic roles; if they struggle, pretraining is likely needed.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add a short “parts overview” (names + simple behaviors) before the full sequence.
- **Best Fix:** Structure the lesson in two stages: component pretraining first, then the causal narrated animation [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].
