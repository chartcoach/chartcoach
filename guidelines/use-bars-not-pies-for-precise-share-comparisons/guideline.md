## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

When comparing shares precisely (especially small differences), use bar or column charts instead of pie or donut charts.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Explain the principle at work here. Connect the rule to human perception or clear communication.

- **The Principle:** Length/position comparisons are easier than comparing angles/arc lengths in circle slices.
- **The Evidence:** The post notes that circle sections “aren't exactly easy to compare,” and that small differences (e.g., 3%) are easy to spot in bars but “practically invisible” in pies/donuts; it also observes election results are almost always shown as bar/column charts [@muth_chart_types_guide_2025].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

This advice is designed for specific moments.

- **User Goal:** Detecting and comparing differences in percentages across categories (e.g., election results, market shares).
- **Data Type:** Part-to-whole percentages across categories.
- **Audience:** Mainstream/general readers [@muth_chart_types_guide_2025].

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

List the specific scenarios where you should ignore this rule.

- **Scenario:** Your main goal is to signal “these are proportions (not absolute numbers)” more than enabling precise comparison.
- **Reason:** The post says pies/donuts make it very obvious you’re talking about percentages and proportions [@muth_chart_types_guide_2025].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

Be honest about the downsides.

- **The Sacrifice:** Bars/columns may feel more familiar/less “fun” than pictorial share charts.
- **The Risk:** If there are many categories, bar charts can become long and require scrolling/space [@muth_chart_types_guide_2025].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

Describe the common anti-patterns or "lazy fixes" people use that don't actually solve the problem.

- **The Wrong Fix:** Using a pie/donut and adding lots of labels to “make it readable.”
- **Why it fails:** Labels don’t fix the core comparison problem—slice sizes are still hard to compare, especially for small differences [@muth_chart_types_guide_2025].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Categories with close percentages look nearly identical in the pie/donut.
- **The Test:** Ask whether a ~3% difference matters to your story; if yes, test a bar chart and see if the difference becomes immediately visible [@muth_chart_types_guide_2025].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Convert the pie/donut into a bar chart sorted by value.
- **Best Fix:** Use a bar chart (or column chart, if space allows) as the primary view for share comparisons, reserving pies/donuts for cases where “part of a whole” signaling is the main goal [@muth_chart_types_guide_2025].
