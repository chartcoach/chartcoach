## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Constrain flexibility in study design, outcome definitions, and analytic modes to minimize biased production of “significant” findings.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The paper defines bias (u) as design/data/analysis/reporting factors that create findings that should not exist and shows PPV decreases as bias increases. Flexibility increases the opportunity to transform negative into positive results (a mechanism for u).

- **The Principle:** Researcher degrees of freedom inflate false “findings” via bias u
- **The Evidence:** [@ioannidisWhyMostPublished2005]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Make reported significant results more likely true
- **Data Type:** Studies with many plausible outcome definitions, subgroups, models, or endpoints
- **Audience:** Investigators, methodologists, journal reviewers

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Method-development contexts where exploring analytic approaches is the main objective
- **Reason:** Flexibility is part of innovation, but claims about relationships should be clearly labeled exploratory. [@ioannidisWhyMostPublished2005]

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Less adaptability to unforeseen data issues; fewer exploratory insights
- **The Risk:** Over-standardization may overlook heterogeneity or misspecification. [@ioannidisWhyMostPublished2005]

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Trying many analyses and only reporting the “best” p-values
- **Why it fails:** This is exactly the bias mechanism (u) that reduces PPV. [@ioannidisWhyMostPublished2005]

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Post hoc subgroup claims, shifting endpoints, or selective reporting patterns
- **The Test:** Ask whether the analysis and outcomes were fixed before looking at results (or whether many alternatives were tried)

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Transparently report all tested outcomes/analyses, not only significant ones
- **Best Fix:** Use a prespecified protocol and adhere to common standards for conduct and reporting to reduce u. [@ioannidisWhyMostPublished2005]
