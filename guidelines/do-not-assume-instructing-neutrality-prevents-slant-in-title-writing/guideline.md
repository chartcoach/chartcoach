## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Add an explicit slant review step for titles; do not rely on “neutral title” instructions to produce neutrality.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Even when explicitly asked to write neutral titles, a substantial portion contained slant, suggesting that prior attitudes and earlier framing can leak into “neutral” phrasing.

- **The Principle:** Bias assimilation in authoring
- **The Evidence:** Only ~70% of “neutral” titles were truly neutral; ~30% contained slant despite bolded “neutral” instructions [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Create titles that minimize unintended editorial bias
- **Data Type:** Politicized/controversial issues where authors have pre-existing attitudes
- **Audience:** Newsroom editors, visualization teams, or platform moderators responsible for title quality [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018]

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Explicit opinion content where slant is declared and expected
- **Reason:** The goal is not neutrality; the goal is transparent advocacy [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** More time and process overhead (reviewers, checks, alternates).
- **The Risk:** Committees may water down clarity by over-correcting perceived slant [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Treating “statistical” phrasing as automatically neutral in review.
- **Why it fails:** Statistics frames can still encode slant via variable/benchmark selection [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** A “neutral” title uses evaluative language or selects only one side’s supporting metric.
- **The Test:** Have a second reviewer categorize the title as supporting/non-supporting/neutral. If disagreement occurs, the title likely contains implicit framing [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Strip evaluative phrasing and reduce to topic + scope.
- **Best Fix:** Create paired alternative titles (supporting and non-supporting) from the same chart and revise toward a balanced or explicitly-scoped neutral title [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].
