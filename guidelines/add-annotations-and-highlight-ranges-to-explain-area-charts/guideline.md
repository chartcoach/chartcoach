## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Add annotations and highlight ranges to area charts to explain key changes and guide interpretation.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Area charts (like line charts) provide enough open space to add explanatory text and highlighted periods. These cues help readers figure out what’s going on and make the chart more engaging to read [@muth_area_charts_2018].

- **The Principle:** Explanatory scaffolding reduces ambiguity in complex time-series displays
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_area_charts_2018]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand why changes happen and what periods matter
- **Data Type:** Area charts where trends or shifts benefit from explanation
- **Audience:** Broad audiences, especially when the topic needs context

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The chart is meant to be a compact, reference-style graphic with no room for extra text.
- **Reason:** Annotations and highlights can crowd the display and reduce its compactness [@muth_area_charts_2018].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Less whitespace and a more editorial (less neutral) feel.
- **The Risk:** Over-annotating can distract from the data or bias attention toward less important features [@muth_area_charts_2018].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Adding long paragraphs instead of targeted annotations or highlights.
- **Why it fails:** Dense text slows reading and can overwhelm the chart rather than guide it [@muth_area_charts_2018].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Readers need verbal explanation to understand what the chart shows, or they miss the key time period.
- **The Test:** Remove your spoken context; if the main takeaway becomes unclear, add a brief annotation or a highlighted range [@muth_area_charts_2018].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add one concise annotation at the most important change point [@muth_area_charts_2018].
- **Best Fix:** Combine a small set of annotations with a highlighted range for the key period so the story is self-explanatory [@muth_area_charts_2018].
