## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->
Include members of the specific target racial or ethnic demographic in the design and review process of educational materials.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->
*   **The Principle:** Cultural Relevance. Different demographic groups may have varying levels of trust in medical systems, different cultural contexts, and different baselines for graph literacy.
*   **The Evidence:** @rodriguez_health_2013 highlights that African Americans may be at a disadvantage when reviewing patient education materials due to literacy disparities. The authors state that "efforts to increase participation of African Americans in the design of educational materials can be an approach to address racial and cultural differences."

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->
*   **User Goal:** Increasing trust and willingness to participate in shared decision-making.
*   **Data Type:** Public health communications or patient decision aids.
*   **Audience:** Specific minority groups or populations with historically lower trust in healthcare institutions.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->
*   **Scenario:** Internal technical audits or machine-to-machine reporting.
*   **Reason:** These contexts do not involve cultural interpretation or patient-facing communication.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->
*   **The Sacrifice:** Time and Logistics. It takes longer to recruit and run design workshops with specific demographic groups than to design in a silo.
*   **The Risk:** Designing "for" a group without them often leads to assumptions that miss the actual barriers to understanding (e.g., layout issues vs. terminology issues).

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->
*   **The Wrong Fix:** Simply translating text into another language without testing the visual format or cultural context.
*   **Why it fails:** It addresses the language barrier but ignores the literacy, numeracy, and graphical literacy barriers identified by @rodriguez_health_2013.

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->
*   **Visual Sign:** N/A (This is a process check).
*   **The Test:** Ask: "Did anyone from the demographic we are targeting actually look at this chart before we published it?"

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->
*   **Quick Fix:** Conduct a "teach-back" session with a small sample of the target audience to check for understanding.
*   **Best Fix:** Integrate community representatives into the design team from the project's inception.
