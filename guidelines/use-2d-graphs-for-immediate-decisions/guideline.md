## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Use 2-D graphs (simple/area) when the goal is correct, immediate use of the information.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Reducing gratuitous depth cues aligns with what viewers in this study judged as best for “right here and now” use, suggesting 2-D is perceived as more straightforward for immediate interpretation.

- **The Principle:** Match perceived appropriateness of dimensionality to time horizon (immediate vs. memory)
- **The Evidence:** In Experiment 2, simple/area graphs were chosen more for “now” than for “memory,” while 3-D volume/surface were chosen more for “memory” than “now” [@levyGratuitousGraphicsPutting1996]. Experiment 3 similarly showed 2-D preferred for “now” scenarios.

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Make an immediate decision correctly; quick comprehension
- **Data Type:** 2-D quantitative series (e.g., values across ordered positions)
- **Audience:** Any viewer; especially when speed and correctness today matter more than recall later

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The primary goal is later recall without revisiting the chart
- **Reason:** Participants preferred 3-D volume graphs for memorability [@levyGratuitousGraphicsPutting1996].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Less “stand out” or perceived impressiveness
- **The Risk:** The display may be judged less memorable or less compelling for presentation contexts

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Adding 3-D shading to “make it pop” for a meeting where a decision must be made immediately
- **Why it fails:** In these studies, 3-D was not the preferred choice for immediate-use scenarios [@levyGratuitousGraphicsPutting1996].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** The chart uses perspective/volume effects even though depth encodes no data
- **The Test:** Ask: “Is this meant to support a decision today?” If yes, try a 2-D version and see if it reads faster at a glance.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Turn off 3-D/perspective effects to produce a 2-D area or simple chart.
- **Best Fix:** Redesign as a clearly 2-D line or bar chart chosen for the task (trend vs. detail), without depth cues [@levyGratuitousGraphicsPutting1996].
