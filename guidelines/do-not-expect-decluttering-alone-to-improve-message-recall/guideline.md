## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

If you need the audience to remember a specific conclusion, add explicit focus (highlight + annotation); do not assume decluttering by itself will increase recall of the intended message.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Decluttering removes distractions but does not necessarily resolve which of many possible patterns should be encoded as “the takeaway.”

- **The Principle:** Reduced clutter ≠ guided interpretation
- **The Evidence:** Ajani et al. found no significant difference in focus-relevant recall between cluttered and decluttered designs for redraw relevance or story-relevant free-response conclusions; the improvements appeared when focus cues were added [@ajaniDeclutterFocusEmpirically2022a].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Ensuring one intended takeaway is recalled after brief viewing
- **Data Type:** Charts with multiple plausible “main points”
- **Audience:** General viewers who may select different salient patterns

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Your goal is simply to improve the look/acceptability of a chart (e.g., professionalism) rather than to steer toward a single message.
- **Reason:** Decluttering did improve professionalism ratings even when it did not change message recall [@ajaniDeclutterFocusEmpirically2022a].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Extra effort to decide and articulate the “one point.”
- **The Risk:** Over-focusing can feel agenda-driven to some viewers [@ajaniDeclutterFocusEmpirically2022a].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Removing chart elements and assuming the “right” pattern will become obvious on its own.
- **Why it fails:** Viewers still notice a wide variety of alternative patterns unless guided [@ajaniDeclutterFocusEmpirically2022a].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** People accurately describe the topic but disagree on the conclusion.
- **The Test:** Collect one-sentence takeaways from a few viewers; high variance indicates declutter-without-focus is insufficient for your communication goal [@ajaniDeclutterFocusEmpirically2022a].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add a headline-like sentence that states the intended conclusion.
- **Best Fix:** Combine that sentence with a single-color highlight of the specific marks that support the conclusion [@ajaniDeclutterFocusEmpirically2022a].
