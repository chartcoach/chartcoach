## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Do not assume graphing calculators will shorten sessions; allocate time explicitly for the additional exploration they enable.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Mesa found that when calculators were available, pairs often spent more total time, with substantial time devoted to exploration—especially in the task where students iteratively tested many functions quickly. The calculator increased the number of trials per minute, which expanded exploration rather than compressing time [@mesaSolvingProblemsFunctions2008].

- **The Principle:** Efficiency gains in one operation can expand the search space and increase time-on-task.
- **The Evidence:** [@mesaSolvingProblemsFunctions2008]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Schedule problem-solving sessions and anticipate pacing with technology.
- **Data Type:** Open-ended or underdetermined tasks where many candidate functions/parameters are plausible.
- **Audience:** Teachers, facilitators, curriculum planners.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The activity is tightly constrained with a short, deterministic sequence of calculator steps (little room for branching).
- **Reason:** Exploration cannot expand if the task design prevents it.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Less coverage of multiple tasks in one class period.
- **The Risk:** Students may spend time on unproductive trial-and-error instead of advancing conceptually [@mesaSolvingProblemsFunctions2008].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Add technology and keep the same time limits expecting “faster work.”
- **Why it fails:** Students use the freed effort to explore more possibilities; without guidance, this may not yield better solutions [@mesaSolvingProblemsFunctions2008].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Students run many graph/table checks and window adjustments; progress stalls despite many trials.
- **The Test:** Compare “number of candidate functions tested” per minute with and without calculators; if it increases sharply, plan for expanded exploration time [@mesaSolvingProblemsFunctions2008].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add interim checkpoints (e.g., “commit to a model by minute 10”) to prevent endless search.
- **Best Fix:** Pair calculator use with concept constraints (e.g., specify required intercepts/zeros) so exploration narrows and time supports reasoning rather than sheer volume of trials [@mesaSolvingProblemsFunctions2008].
