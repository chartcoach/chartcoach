## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Use the simplest depiction that supports the decision task; remove realistic detail that is not task-relevant.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The review summarizes evidence that people prefer realistic or high-quality images and may judge them as more credible, yet such realism can increase time, draw attention to irrelevant details, and harm task performance—even for domain professionals in some cases [@padillaDecisionMakingVisualizations2018].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Relative-position judgments, route tasks, locating features, comparing values over space
- **Data Type:** Geospatial displays, scientific figures, medical/scientific communication graphics
- **Audience:** General users and novices; also contexts where “looking scientific” is tempting

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Shape-understanding tasks where realistic depiction is the task-relevant information
- **Reason:** If realism encodes the needed shape cues, simplification can remove necessary information [@padillaDecisionMakingVisualizations2018].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Aesthetic appeal and perceived “professionalism”
- **The Risk:** Users may distrust or undervalue a simpler display even if it performs better [@padillaDecisionMakingVisualizations2018].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Adding 3D or photorealistic styling to “increase credibility”
- **Why it fails:** It can trigger credibility biases while worsening attention allocation and performance [@padillaDecisionMakingVisualizations2018].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users spend time on textures, terrain, shading, or decorative features unrelated to the question.
- **The Test:** Ask which visual elements were used to answer; if answers cite irrelevant realistic details, realism is interfering [@padillaDecisionMakingVisualizations2018].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Remove or mute non-essential layers (textures, shading, decorative 3D effects).
- **Best Fix:** Re-encode only task-relevant variables with clear, minimal marks and validate with task-based performance tests [@padillaDecisionMakingVisualizations2018].
