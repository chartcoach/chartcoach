## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

When communicating risk reduction and your priority is comprehension, present absolute risk reduction (ARR) rather than number needed to treat (NNT).

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

ARR was better understood than NNT in the evidence base, suggesting ARR more directly supports correct interpretation of effect size than the reciprocal-based NNT format.

- **The Principle:** Direct-difference interpretability
- **The Evidence:** ARR vs NNT improved understanding (SMD 0.42, 95% CI 0.12 to 0.71) in [@aklUsingAlternativeStatistical2011].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Correctly estimate or interpret the magnitude of a treatment effect.
- **Data Type:** Risk-reduction summaries for interventions where NNT is an alternative display.
- **Audience:** Consumers (the understanding comparison came from a consumer study in [@aklUsingAlternativeStatistical2011]).

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Your primary goal is not understanding but matching a professional convention that requires NNT display.
- **Reason:** The review’s evidence supports understanding differences; it does not show that ARR is always the required reporting standard in all settings [@aklUsingAlternativeStatistical2011].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** You lose the “how many to treat for one to benefit” framing some clinicians expect.
- **The Risk:** ARR can feel small and may be perceived as less compelling than other formats.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Showing NNT alone as the only measure of benefit.
- **Why it fails:** NNT was less well understood than ARR in [@aklUsingAlternativeStatistical2011], and can be misread without baseline context.

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Benefit is reported only as “NNT = X” with no absolute risk difference.
- **The Test:** Ask a reader “out of 1000 treated, how many benefit?” If they cannot infer it from the display, comprehension may suffer.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add ARR alongside NNT (e.g., “ARR 2% (NNT 50)”).
- **Best Fix:** Show control risk and treatment risk, plus ARR; include NNT only as a secondary translation for those who want it, consistent with the comparative findings in [@aklUsingAlternativeStatistical2011].
