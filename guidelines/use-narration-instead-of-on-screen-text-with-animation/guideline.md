## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

When explaining an animation, use spoken narration for the words instead of concurrent on-screen text.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Spoken words and visual animation can be processed in separate channels; moving words off the screen reduces overload in the visual channel so learners can select and process key visual information.

- **The Principle:** Off-loading / Modality effect
- **The Evidence:** Learners show better transfer from animation+narrration than animation+on-screen text [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand how a process/system works (build a mental model and transfer it).
- **Data Type:** Causal, step-based explanations shown with animation or dynamic graphics.
- **Audience:** Especially learners facing unfamiliar material or fast-paced presentations.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** There is no animation (or no competing visual stream).
- **Reason:** The redundancy concern is tied to competition for visual processing; the paper notes cases where adding on-screen text can help when it does not compete with animation [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Users can’t scan or re-read the wording on screen.
- **The Risk:** If audio is missed (noise, distraction), comprehension can drop.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Showing full on-screen sentences while also narrating them during animation.
- **Why it fails:** It creates split attention/visual overload and can trigger unnecessary processing to reconcile streams [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Users must constantly shift between reading and watching, or the text forces the animation into the background.
- **The Test:** If the viewer cannot follow the animation without pausing to read, the visual channel is likely overloaded.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Remove the on-screen sentences and narrate them.
- **Best Fix:** Redesign the explanation as narrated animation where the screen is reserved primarily for the visuals [@mayerNineWaysReduce2003].
