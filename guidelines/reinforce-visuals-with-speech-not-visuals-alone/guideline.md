## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

For any critical fact, state, or action shown visually, include a corresponding reference in the speech track.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

In recall testing, propositions conveyed only by image/animation without speech cueing were poorly recalled (e.g., initial DNA state; complex formation; detaching). Well-recalled propositions were typically conveyed by animation and reinforced by speech [@faradayDesigningEffectiveMultimedia1997].

- **The Principle:** Reinforcement improves encoding; prevents unattended visual-only meaning
- **The Evidence:** [@faradayDesigningEffectiveMultimedia1997]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Remember the steps and causal relations of a process.
- **Data Type:** Instructional or explanatory multimedia sequences.
- **Audience:** General and novice audiences.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The presentation must be accessible without audio.
- **Reason:** Speech reinforcement is not available; use text/captions instead.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Longer narration; risk of verbosity.
- **The Risk:** If narration is not tightly tied to visuals, it can distract rather than reinforce.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Assuming “it’s obvious in the animation” and omitting narration.
- **Why it fails:** Viewers may look at the object but not infer the intended proposition, reducing recall [@faradayDesigningEffectiveMultimedia1997].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Viewers describe what moved but not what it meant or caused.
- **The Test:** Proposition-based recall: if visual-only propositions are consistently missing, speech reinforcement is insufficient.

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add a short narrated clause naming the visual action/state as it occurs.
- **Best Fix:** Script narration as a pointer to on-screen entities and actions, aligning timing with the animation beats [@faradayDesigningEffectiveMultimedia1997].
