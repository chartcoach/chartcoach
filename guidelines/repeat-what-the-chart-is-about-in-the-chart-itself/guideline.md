## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Make the chart self-orienting by repeating key context words (subject, unit, and what’s being measured) inside the chart, not only in the title/description.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Readers often “dive right in” and treat the description as a fallback; repeating the subject and units within the chart reduces confusion and keeps comprehension on-track without forcing extra reading steps [@muth_readers_time_2017].

- **The Principle:** Redundant orientation cues
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_readers_time_2017]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Immediately understand what is being measured (and in what units) during a fast scan.
- **Data Type:** Charts where the metric could be misread without cues (e.g., “area” vs. “temperature”), and where units matter.
- **Audience:** Skimmers and non-experts who may not read captions unless stuck [@muth_readers_time_2017].

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Extremely space-constrained layouts where extra text will obscure the data.
- **Reason:** Redundancy can crowd the plot area and reduce legibility, undermining the goal of clarity [@muth_readers_time_2017].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** More text and design time (finding good wording, placing it cleanly).
- **The Risk:** Repetition can feel heavy or cluttered if not integrated carefully [@muth_readers_time_2017].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Assuming the title/description is sufficient orientation.
- **Why it fails:** Many readers won’t read it until they’re already confused; orientation arrives too late [@muth_readers_time_2017].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Without reading the description, it’s unclear what the chart measures or what units mean.
- **The Test:** Cover the title/description and ask: can you tell what’s being measured and in what unit from axis labels and in-chart wording alone? If not, add redundancy [@muth_readers_time_2017].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add unit and measure cues on axes (e.g., include “sq. km,” “size,” or the measured concept) and add one in-chart phrase that repeats the subject [@muth_readers_time_2017].
- **Best Fix:** Thread the subject language throughout the annotations and labels (including synonyms where appropriate) so readers are continuously reminded what they’re looking at [@muth_readers_time_2017].
