## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Do not assign a strong blue to Europe or to categories representing white people; use blue for another category or avoid strong blue altogether.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Strong blue is culturally associated with professionalism/competence/royalty; pairing it with Europe or white people can reinforce notions of superiority.

- **The Principle:** Avoid hierarchical connotations in categorical color assignment
- **The Evidence:** [@muth_race_ethnicity_colors_2024]

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Compare regions or racial groups without signaling status differences
- **Data Type:** World-region maps/charts or race/ethnicity breakdowns where one category could be read as “dominant”
- **Audience:** General audiences

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Blue is unavoidable due to template/brand constraints
- **Reason:** The post suggests mitigation strategies (shift hue, distribute blue across groups) when blue is a must [@muth_race_ethnicity_colors_2024].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** You give up a popular, high-contrast, widely liked categorical color.
- **The Risk:** Alternative hues may be less familiar for region coding, requiring clearer legends and labels.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Keeping Europe/white people in the most saturated, “premium” blue while giving others duller colors
- **Why it fails:** It intensifies the perceived hierarchy the rule is trying to avoid [@muth_race_ethnicity_colors_2024].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Europe/white people is the only category in a vivid, saturated blue while others are muted or warm.
- **The Test:** Swap the blue with another category in a draft; if the chart’s “status feel” changes noticeably, your original mapping likely carried unintended connotations [@muth_race_ethnicity_colors_2024].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Reassign blue to a different category and give Europe/white people a different hue of similar prominence.
- **Best Fix:** If blue is required, shift it toward purple/turquoise or use multiple blues across several categories so no single group “owns” blue [@muth_race_ethnicity_colors_2024].
