## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->
Do not assume that chaotic or "playful" layouts are more engaging or aesthetically pleasing. Choose structured layouts (like columns or radial lists) to maximize both performance and perceived visual appeal.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->
There is a misconception that the "messiness" of a word cloud is what makes it engaging. However, usability testing reveals that users rate structured, semantically organized layouts higher on aesthetics, readability, and engagement than traditional chaotic designs.
*   **The Principle:** Aesthetic-Usability Effect
*   **The Evidence:** In subjective ratings, 93% of participants preferred Column layouts for functionality, and 83% preferred them for aesthetics over standard Wordle layouts [@hearst_evaluation_2020]. Users described structured views as "cleaner" and chaotic ones as "messy" or "cramped" [@hearst_evaluation_2020].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->
*   **User Goal:** Assessing content quality or engagement (e.g., advertisements, course descriptions).
*   **Data Type:** Any text visualization.
*   **Audience:** General public and stakeholders.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->
*   **Scenario:** Branding or "Tech-Art."
*   **Reason:** If the visual must signal "generative art" or "complexity" rather than "information," a chaotic layout might fit a specific stylistic brand guideline better than a structured list.

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->
*   **The Sacrifice:** The "Cloud" metaphor. Structured layouts look less like clouds and more like infographics.
*   **The Risk:** You may need to manually curate or algorithmically sort data, which is harder than dumping text into a standard generator.

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->
*   **The Wrong Fix:** Rotating words in various directions to make a structured list look "fun."
*   **Why it fails:** This reduces readability without effectively adding the "playfulness" that designers hope for, and users may still prefer the cleaner version [@hearst_evaluation_2020].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->
*   **Visual Sign:** Are words scattered in random orientations and positions?
*   **The Test:** Ask users, "Which design looks more professional?" or "Which design makes you want to read the content?"

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->
*   **Quick Fix:** Align all text horizontally.
*   **Best Fix:** Adopt a structured layout (columns or grouped lists) that reflects the underlying data structure.
