## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

Do not rely on “it’s just statistics” wording to signal neutrality; explicitly check and disclose how the title frames the story.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

Participants often judged visualizations as neutral because they contained statistics, even when their recalled “main message” matched the title’s slant—showing a gap between perceived impartiality and actual framing impact.

- **The Principle:** Over-trust in quantified displays + unnoticed framing
- **The Evidence:** ~80% rated information as neutral regardless of attitude-consistency, yet title slant significantly influenced perceived main message; respondents frequently justified neutrality by “it is statistics” [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Avoid misleading readers who equate numbers with neutrality
- **Data Type:** Controversial-topic visualizations where selective statistics can support opposing sides
- **Audience:** General audiences in news/social media contexts [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018]

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** Internal expert dashboards where the audience already understands metric choices and context
- **Reason:** Experts may be less likely to confuse “quantitative” with “unframed,” though the paper primarily studies general audiences [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Added editorial effort (reviewing alternative framings, documenting choices).
- **The Risk:** Readers may react negatively when framing is made explicit (because it reveals subjectivity) [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Declaring “neutral” because the title includes a numeric comparison.
- **Why it fails:** Numeric comparisons can still be selectively chosen (e.g., which countries/benchmarks are mentioned), producing slant while appearing objective [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** The title cites numbers but omits important context (alternative benchmark/variable also present in chart).
- **The Test:** Generate an alternative title from the same chart that supports the opposing stance. If both feel defensible, your current title is a framing choice that should be treated as such [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add clarifying scope/benchmark statements to the title (what comparison, which metric).
- **Best Fix:** Provide balanced titling or enable readers to see multiple plausible titles/framings for the same chart (e.g., exposing alternative titles) [@kongFramesSlantsTitles2018].
