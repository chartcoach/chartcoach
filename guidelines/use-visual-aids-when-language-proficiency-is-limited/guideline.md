## The Rule <!-- role: advice -->

When your audience may have limited proficiency in the language of the message, include icon arrays alongside the numerical risk information.

## The Logic <!-- role: reason -->

The review reports that denominator neglect and inaccurate risk-reduction estimates were more pronounced when information was presented in a nonnative language, and that icon arrays reduced these errors—especially in the nonnative-language condition, where participants may rely more on the visual display than on the verbal description [@garcia-retameroUsingVisualAids2012].

## Where to Apply <!-- role: context -->

- **User Goal:** Understand treatment benefit/harm from communicated study outcomes.
- **Data Type:** Ratios for treated vs. untreated outcomes.
- **Audience:** Immigrants, multilingual populations, or any group receiving risk information in a nonnative language.

## When to Break It <!-- role: exceptions -->

- **Scenario:** The setting allows delivering the information fully in the audience’s native language with adequate time and support.
- **Reason:** The paper indicates language barriers amplify errors; removing the barrier can reduce reliance on visuals as compensation [@garcia-retameroUsingVisualAids2012].

## The Price <!-- role: costs -->

- **The Sacrifice:** Additional design effort and layout space.
- **The Risk:** If the icon array is poorly matched to the numbers, users may trust the picture and disregard text, worsening comprehension [@garcia-retameroUsingVisualAids2012].

## Common Mistakes <!-- role: mistakes -->

- **The Wrong Fix:** Translating text but leaving risk information in complex numeric-only form.
- **Why it fails:** The review suggests language proficiency interacts with numeracy demands; visuals can reduce the processing burden when language is a barrier [@garcia-retameroUsingVisualAids2012].

## How to Check <!-- role: check -->

- **Visual Sign:** Risk information is conveyed primarily through prose and ratios.
- **The Test:** Ask nonnative speakers to explain the difference between options; if explanations focus on raw counts or are inconsistent, add visual support [@garcia-retameroUsingVisualAids2012].

## How to Fix <!-- role: fix -->

- **Quick Fix:** Add icon arrays that depict each option’s denominator and numerator.
- **Best Fix:** Use paired icon arrays with minimal reliance on prose, so the core comparison can be made visually even when language comprehension is limited [@garcia-retameroUsingVisualAids2012].
