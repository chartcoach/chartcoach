Labels are the catalog's controlled vocabulary. Use them when you have a chart type, task, audience, or design action in mind.

Search finds words. Labels follow the label families defined in `MANIFEST.md`.

## Format

Labels use either `family:category` or `family:category:modifier`:

- `chart:bar`
- `task:compare`
- `audience:novice`
- `component:legend:avoid`

The parser trims surrounding whitespace from the label and each segment. Segment text remains case-sensitive.

## Matching

On the guidelines listing page:

- Click a label to filter the list in the browser.
- Add more labels to tighten the result set.
- Choose a family such as `chart` to match every `chart:*` label.
- Choose a full label such as `chart:bar` to match that exact value.

## Use

- Start broad with a family label.
- Add full labels for a specific chart, task, or audience.
- Use search first when you remember a phrase from the guideline.

Use labels after search when related guidelines need to be separated by chart, task, audience, or design concern.
