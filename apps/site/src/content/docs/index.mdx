## What this is

Chart Coach is an experiment in representing visualization design knowledge in a format that is:

- human-readable and easy to author,
- machine-usable for retrieval and reasoning,
- context-aware and linkable,
- traceable to sources.

This site is the public-facing view of the catalog: browse guidelines, scan their labels, and follow citations to the underlying evidence.
